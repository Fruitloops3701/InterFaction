FAST REFERENCE

A calm order of
operations.

  1. 01
    Scan the whole intersection.

    Look for signs, signals, people walking, cyclists, and the paths of other vehicles.

  2. 02
    Obey the traffic control.

    A police officer, signal, or sign comes before the usual right-of-way rules.

  3. 03
    Yield before you turn across traffic.

    Especially when turning left, wait for a safe gap and a clear crosswalk.

  4. 04
    When in doubt, let it go.

    Right of way is given, not taken. A few seconds is always worth the margin.
